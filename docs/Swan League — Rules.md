## Overview

The Swan League is a team-based NFL pick'em pool — no players, no lineups, no waivers. Each entrant submitted 9 NFL teams at the start of the season.

## Scoring

Each week, you start **3 of your 9** teams. If a started team wins, you score its point value. A loss or tie scores zero — no partial credit, no margin of victory.

Point values are set by where each team finished in its **2025 division**:

| 2025 finish | Points per win |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 1 |
| 2nd | 2 |
| 3rd | 3 |
| 4th | 4 |

The inversion is the core mechanic: a bad team that wins is worth up to 4x a good team that wins.

**Perfect Week bonus:** +3 points if all 3 team picks win in a given week.

## Intra-roster collisions

Lions vs. Vikings (x2) and 49ers vs. Rams (x2) — four weeks where two of your own teams play each other and one is guaranteed to lose.

## The weekly dog

One underdog pick per week — any team without the minus sign, not limited to your own roster. The dog pick must be submitted before that specific game kicks off (a Monday-night dog buys time until Monday night).

**Double-dog weeks** (two dog picks): Week 2, Thanksgiving week, Christmas week.

## Deadlines and fines

- Team picks: **Sunday 1:00 PM ET**, via Google form.
- Missed submission: **$5 fine**.
- No reminders from the commissioner — picks aren't viewed until after the deadline, so a missed pick is invisible until it's too late.

## The money ($100 buy-in)

| Pool | Stake | Payout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main (most total points) | $60 | 60/25/10/5 |
| Dog (most dog points) | $20 | 1st, 2nd, 3rd |
| Regular season (points, no dogs) | $10 | 1st, 2nd |
| Playoffs (most playoff points) | $10 | 1st, 2nd |

### Playoff multipliers

Applied to a team's regular point value:

| Round | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Wild Card | x2 |
| Divisional | x4 |
| Conference | x6 |
| Super Bowl | x8 |

Plus **3 points per team just for qualifying** for the playoffs.

Example: a 4-point team winning a Divisional-round game scores 16 points; a Super Bowl win scores 32.

### Records

- **"Going Perf":** all 4 picks win in a week (3 team starts + the dog).
- **"0fer":** all 4 picks lose in a week.
- The fine pool (from missed-deadline fines) splits 50/50 between the season's Going Perf and 0fer record holders.
