# Swan League — Weekly Starter Method

Sep 26, 2026

## The formula

Pick your 3 weekly starters (out of your 9-team roster) using this process:

1. **Pull current moneylines** for each available (non-bye) team.
2. **Convert moneyline → implied win probability**, then de-vig it.
3. **Multiply win probability × the team's point value** (1–4, based on 2025 division finish). This gives an EV ranking number.
4. **Start the 3 teams with the highest EV.**

EV is a **ranking tool only** — not a projected score. You get 2, 3, 4, or 0 points on Sunday, nothing in between.

## Tiebreaker

If two candidates are within \~0.05 EV of each other, use **perfect-week probability** instead: multiply all three teams' win% together — higher product wins the slot.

Example: in Week 3, this flipped the 49ers over the Saints for the third starting slot — 48.9% vs 38.6% perfect-week odds, worth more expected points than the 0.13 raw-EV gap the Saints held.

## Rules of thumb

- A 2-point team beats a 3-pointer when it wins \~1.5x as often; beats a 4-pointer at \~2x.
- Never autopilot to the highest point value — a 4-point road dog often loses to a 3-point home favorite.
- On collisions (two of your own teams playing each other), start whichever side the line favors — never bench both.
